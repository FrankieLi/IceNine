Pilot (2026-10-06): 2 voxels (7336, 3108) x 10 radii x 20 directions x 2 variants, all pipelines,
10 workers, net realistic_s0. Full tables: pilot_full.txt (benchmarks/nn_hybrid/).

Checks against the stored sweeps
- N1 / N3 (net x1 / x3 rebuilt by the copied pass loop): max |err_angle - perturbation_sweep_raw| =
  2.3e-07 / 1.7e-07 deg over 787 cases (float32 storage).
- H0 (FindOptimal alone, our images + B's seed): R_final bit-identical to findoptimal_b_raw in
  800/800 cases; mean evaluations per case equal (1559, realistic).
- NOTE: findoptimal_b_raw (and the hybrid raws) store the voxel axis SORTED by voxel index, while
  the perturbation / optimizer / A raws are in sweep order; summary.py and the tests re-index.
  (The first version of the exactness test compared the wrong voxel for this reason.)
- HG's net starts at the Huber estimate, so its N1/N3 are not compared with the sweep's.

Sanity of the results (2 voxels only, medians in deg)
- clean: net x3 median ~0.005 and FindOptimal returns the start (H3 == N3 to 3 digits); H0 (FO
  alone) 0.024-0.18 up to r = 1.5, wrong 15-100% for r >= 1.5. The hybrid never makes it worse.
- realistic: N3 0.07-0.12, H0 0.019-0.18 (r <= 1.5), H3 0.020-0.033 for r <= 3 (below the net's
  0.06 floor), H1 close to H3. r = 5: all net-based methods fail except HG (median 0.024).
- H3c (covariance box) worse than H3 where the box exceeds the default; H3m (MC, box 3 sigma)
  worse than H3 by 0.01-0.06 deg.

Timings (10 workers, contended; mean s per case, realistic): net stage prepare 0.14 + render 0.07 +

2302; HG 2.43 s / 3773 (+ GN 0.25 s). Almost all finisher time is cost evaluations (~0.7 ms each),
